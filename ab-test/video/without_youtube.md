## Description

He always said "later."

A little boy has a red kite and one wish: to fly it on the hill with his dad. But there is always one more email, one more call, one more raised finger. Seasons pass. The boy stops asking. Then, one day, the father finally has time… and his grown-up son answers him the same way.

LATER is a wordless animated short about the one thing we can never get back: time with the people who are waiting for us.

One day, "later" runs out. Make time for the ones who wait for you.
