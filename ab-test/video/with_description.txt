Every night Leo types "Hey Dad"… and deletes it. Across town, his father does exactly the same.
Then a stranger on a park bench made one phone call.

A wordless animated short film about drifting apart from a parent, waiting for the "right words", and how little it takes to come back.

💬 Who is the one person you keep meaning to text? Maybe today is the day. 💬
